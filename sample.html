Inline and display math

Euler's identity is e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0, and the mass-energy equivalence is E = mc^2. The Gaussian integral is a display equation:

\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\, dx = \sqrt{\pi}

A sum with limits:

\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i = x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_n
